# Luna Network - strategy guide

[Player handbook](../StrategyGuide.md) · [Matchups](../Matchups.md) · [Full mechanics](../../LunaNetwork/README.md)

**Leader:** GPT-5.6 Luna  
**Playstyle:** Wide expansion and construction tempo  
**Suggested victories:** Science or Domination; flexible conventional victories

Turn a network of useful cities into a stream of completed orders. Discounts and completion refunds reward sustained development; Rapid Response helps newly trained forces reach the point of need.

## Opening priorities

Escort Packet Settlers toward defensible food and resource sites. Cheap Cache Nodes help new cities become productive research centres, but ordinary Happiness and maintenance still determine how many cities you can support.

## Develop the engine

Keep normal production queues moving through needed buildings and combat units. Time military completions before a deployment; purchases and free units do not create the same refund engine. Use roads and a reserve army to cover a wide border.

## Convert the advantage

Convert the developed network into research capacity or several coordinated production centres. Reinforce campaigns continuously instead of assuming the temporary movement promotion is a permanent combat bonus.

## Pros

- Discounted settlement and early research infrastructure help establish more viable cities.
- Many qualifying completions repeatedly feed the next order.
- Fresh land and naval combat units have a short deployment advantage.

## Cons and opportunity costs

- There is no built-in Happiness or growth solution for overexpansion.
- Refunds require qualifying production completions rather than purchases or Wonders.
- Rapid Response adds tempo, not a general strength advantage.

## Playing against this civ

- Contest strong settlement sites and attack exposed new cities before their infrastructure pays back.
- Force military spending and road defense across several approaches.
- Survive the fresh-unit deployment window and fight with terrain or stronger units.

## Common mistakes

- Expanding faster than food, Gold and Happiness can support.
- Expecting Packet Settlers, Wonders or purchases to generate military completion refunds.
- Counting on Rapid Response after its short duration has expired.
