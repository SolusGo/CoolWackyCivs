# Eternal Number Ten - strategy guide

[Player handbook](../StrategyGuide.md) · [Matchups](../Matchups.md) · [Full mechanics](../../EternalNumberTen/README.md)

**Leader:** Lionel Messi  
**Playstyle:** Great People, diplomacy and a permanent career progression  
**Suggested victories:** Culture or Diplomacy; Science through specialists

Build a reliable stream of defining achievements and keep the career moving through both Legacy and Era gates. Use formation play to support the economy, rather than making every chapter depend on risky fighting.

## Opening priorities

Establish food and early Culture, explore for contacts, and prepare cities that can support specialists. Choose achievable Great Person and diplomatic milestones; a chapter requiring a later Era cannot be rushed by Legacy alone.

## Develop the engine

Use La Masia in genuine specialist cities and keep the Number Ten protected near coordinated land forces. Position the formation before turn start to receive One-Two Football, and take eligible Assists without exposing the support unit.

## Convert the advantage

Convert permanent career benefits into Tourism, Great Works and alliances. Bank a coherent route to late chapters instead of pursuing every Wonder. After the final chapter, further Legacy has its own bounded repeat rewards.

## Pros

- Several peaceful and military achievements contribute to one permanent progression.
- La Masia supports specialists, Great People and local growth.
- Formation bonuses and the Number Ten improve tactical positioning.

## Cons and opportunity costs

- Later chapters need specific Eras as well as sufficient Legacy.
- Great People, Wonders and alliances compete with ordinary economic needs.
- Formation and Assist rewards require real positioning and eligibility.

## Playing against this civ

- Contest accessible Wonder and City-State opportunities without feeding easy Assists.
- Use pressure that forces the specialist cities to divert resources into defense.
- Separate the support General from the formation and finish exposed units decisively.

## Common mistakes

- Expecting banked Legacy to bypass an Era gate.
- Leaving the Number Ten exposed because its movement is excellent.
- Treating every nominal kill as an eligible Assist or unlimited reward source.
