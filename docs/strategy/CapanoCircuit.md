# The Capano Circuit - strategy guide

[Player handbook](../StrategyGuide.md) · [Matchups](../Matchups.md) · [Full mechanics](../../CapanoCircuit/README.md)

**Leader:** Enrico Capano  
**Playstyle:** Patient combat projects and terrain-trained veterans  
**Suggested victories:** Domination or Science/Culture from successful development

Use strong terrain and support to survive repeated attacks on a worthy target, then convert a genuine four-Beta finish into a SEND. Your economy also needs developed Boulder Sectors; combat rewards are conditional.

## Opening priorities

Scout Hills and Mountain-adjacent land for legal, nonadjacent Boulder Sectors. Protect Route Setters and build a normal mixed army rather than sacrificing early units in an attempt to manufacture a SEND.

## Develop the engine

Choose a durable Project that the same attacker can safely revisit. Maintain ranged support, healing and an escape route while building Beta. A weaker initial target or a different target will not deliver the intended qualifying finish.

## Convert the advantage

Preserve successful climbers and their permanent techniques. Combine upgraded Sectors and training with carefully chosen assaults; a defended city can be a valuable Project only if the attacker survives long enough to finish it.

## Pros

- Qualifying SENDs connect combat success to healing, experience and economic rewards.
- Permanent techniques make surviving specialists increasingly useful.
- Boulder Sectors add an independent development and training plan.

## Cons and opportunity costs

- Four-Beta rewards require repeated survival and an initially worthy target.
- Changing the target abandons the existing Project.
- Terrain requirements constrain Sector placement and training access.

## Playing against this civ

- Rotate or withdraw a marked defender rather than allowing the same safe attack repeatedly.
- Focus the Project attacker before it can heal through a finish.
- Use fast decisive attacks to deny the patient combat sequence.

## Common mistakes

- Farming weak targets and expecting a qualifying SEND.
- Changing targets just before the fourth-Beta finish.
- Trading away a developed climber for a city that ordinary support could secure.
