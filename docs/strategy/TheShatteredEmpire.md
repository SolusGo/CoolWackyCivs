# Shattered Empire - strategy guide

[Player handbook](../StrategyGuide.md) · [Matchups](../Matchups.md) · [Full mechanics](../../TheShatteredEmpire/README.md)

**Leader:** The Last Emperor  
**Playstyle:** A strong initial footprint constrained by provincial politics  
**Suggested victories:** Domination or Science after administrative consolidation

Use the starting imperial footprint to establish a lead while preventing Authority and provincial Loyalty from collapsing. Three early settlements are an opportunity to build a connected state, not an exemption from maintenance and garrison costs.

## Opening priorities

Inspect whether the provincial entitlements produced cities or fallback Settlers. Connect and defend the provinces, improve food, and manage the treasury. Keep the starting Authority advantage rather than spending it on avoidable losses.

## Develop the engine

Read each Governor's actual factors and demands. Build Palaces and viable connections, match garrisons to vulnerable provinces, and expand within the administration you can sustain. High Ambition is not automatically bad when Loyalty is healthy.

## Convert the advantage

Use loyal provinces to support research or a concentrated campaign. During civil war, choose suppression or negotiation according to military and political resources; diplomatic reunification can restore the empire but does not promise the same Authority reward as military success.

## Pros

- Starting provincial entitlements create early geographic and economic options.
- Governor personalities can specialize local output.
- Imperial management offers both military and negotiated recovery routes.

## Cons and opportunity costs

- Provinces impose maintenance and loyalty-management costs.
- City and capital losses damage Authority.
- Overextension, long wars and neglected demands can turn an early lead into fragmentation.

## Playing against this civ

- Target exposed provinces and connections instead of only the main imperial stack.
- Force extended commitments when Loyalty and administration are under strain.
- Exploit a real succession or civil-war weakness rather than assuming three starting cities imply endless early strength.

## Common mistakes

- Leaving a newly created province without improvements, a connection or protection.
- Treating a gifted fallback Settler as an obligation to found an indefensible city immediately.
- Expecting negotiated-only reunification to award the full military Authority bonus.
