# The Relentless Seven - strategy guide

[Player handbook](../StrategyGuide.md) · [Matchups](../Matchups.md) · [Full mechanics](../../CristianoRonaldo/README.md)

**Leader:** Cristiano Ronaldo  
**Playstyle:** Preserved military veterans and a seven-chapter career  
**Suggested victories:** Domination or Culture; Diplomacy as a secondary route

Develop military experience, survive genuine upgrades and earn Ambition through several achievement types. Reinvention rewards a unit's own career; buying an advanced replacement does not recreate the veteran that was lost.

## Opening priorities

Build a real defensive army and Sporting Academies in worthwhile training cities. Fight controlled battles, keep promotion candidates alive, and support ordinary Culture and economic development.

## Develop the engine

Save Gold for genuine upgrades and pursue achievable Ambition sources alongside combat. Let ranged units soften targets before valuable finishers attack. Read both chapter progress and the current Reinvention cap before planning around a later unlock.

## Convert the advantage

Use upgraded veterans and permanent career benefits for a focused campaign or a stronger cultural economy. Keep the capital and support cities functioning; experience and kill healing are valuable only on units that survive the enemy's full turn.

## Pros

- Combat XP and genuine upgrades reward long-lived armies.
- Several military and peaceful achievements contribute Ambition.
- Reinvention combines bounded permanent strength with healing on kills.

## Cons and opportunity costs

- Lost veterans cannot be replaced with their accumulated career history.
- Reinvention has a chapter-dependent cap.
- Several rewards require genuine combat, production or birth events rather than arbitrary creation or repeated manipulation.

## Playing against this civ

- Focus a valuable veteran to completion instead of spreading damage across heal-on-kill targets.
- Deny easy wounded-unit finishes and force expensive upgrade choices.
- Apply pressure before the later chapters and avoid feeding a long sequence of successful engagements.

## Common mistakes

- Expecting a newly built advanced unit to receive earlier Reinvention tiers.
- Treating every conversion or promotion change as a genuine upgrade.
- Taking a risky extra kill when retreat would preserve an irreplaceable veteran.
