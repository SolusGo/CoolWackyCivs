# The Last City - strategy guide

[Player handbook](../StrategyGuide.md) · [Matchups](../Matchups.md) · [Full mechanics](../../TheLastCity/README.md)

**Leader:** The Warden  
**Playstyle:** One-city survival and siege preparation  
**Suggested victories:** Humanity Endures survival objective; conventional victory remains separate

Keep Last Light fed, housed and defensible through recurring invasions. Refugees, Provisions and Morale are real capacity decisions; the Dawn Initiative is a survival progression rather than an automatic standard Civilization victory.

## Opening priorities

Found a defensible Last Light with useful food and production. Establish the Sanctuary District and a mixed defensive army. Check Housing and Provisions before accepting additional pressure on the population.

## Develop the engine

Use the Council to balance refugees, rationing and infrastructure with the invasion schedule. Preserve participating Last Watch veterans, maintain ranged damage and repair capacity, and keep a reserve for a difficult Major Siege.

## Convert the advantage

Prepare the documented Dawn Initiative requirements and a viable Final Night defense before spending the resources. After Humanity Endures, use the expanded survival phase deliberately; an ordinary Science or Culture objective still needs its normal requirements.

## Pros

- Local defensive bonuses reward a compact, well-positioned army.
- Surviving Major Sieges develops the city and eligible veterans.
- A single concentrated economic and defensive plan reduces ordinary expansion logistics.

## Cons and opportunity costs

- Permanent expansion and ordinary conquest are deliberately restricted.
- One city's food, Housing, Provisions and Morale are strategic bottlenecks.
- Success at the custom survival objective does not itself fulfill a normal victory condition.

## Playing against this civ

- Pressure the capital's productive tiles and force resource spending before a siege.
- Use ranged support and sustained economic denial rather than feeding units into strong local defenses.
- A conventional research or cultural race can demand a response that pure city defense does not supply.

## Common mistakes

- Accepting refugees without considering capacity and supplies.
- Assuming every newly trained Watch inherits old siege ranks.
- Launching the final challenge as soon as a button is available without a prepared defense.
