# The First Night - strategy guide

[Player handbook](../StrategyGuide.md) · [Matchups](../Matchups.md) · [Full mechanics](../../PaulsoaresJr/README.md)

**Leader:** PaulsoaresJr  
**Playstyle:** Exploration and early memories with later recall  
**Suggested victories:** Science or Culture; flexible peaceful development

Acquire different Memories early and preserve the exploration units that can safely earn them. The lasting payoff comes from the acquisition Era and later recall, not from repeating the same event.

## Opening priorities

Scout for contact, a safe Barbarian opportunity and distant discoveries while building the first shelter. Improve genuine resource sites and expand when the economy can support it. An early Memory can pay again in several later Eras.

## Develop the engine

Check which Memories remain unearned rather than continuing to chase one already recorded. Keep a Survivor's return-home route open and protect its experience; ordinary city infrastructure must carry the economy between recall events.

## Convert the advantage

Use accumulated Culture, Science and a developed capital to pursue the chosen victory. Later-era recall is support for a real research or cultural programme, not an endlessly repeatable substitute for it.

## Pros

- Several ordinary exploration and development actions have early rewards.
- Older Memories become more valuable at later Era transitions.
- Survivors provide an exploration and preservation theme without a required war.

## Cons and opportunity costs

- Each Memory can be earned only once.
- Map access and safe discovery routes affect the opening opportunities.
- There is no general combat-strength engine to protect a careless scout or weak border.

## Playing against this civ

- Control important approaches and contested settlement sites without donating easy camp or unit rewards.
- Punish isolated Survivors and cut their safe return paths.
- Pressure the economy between Era transitions rather than allowing uninterrupted peaceful development.

## Common mistakes

- Waiting until later Eras to complete easy early Memories.
- Repeating a trigger and expecting another permanent Memory.
- Sending a veteran far away without a legal, defensible way home.
