# Rou'ls Ascendancy - strategy guide

[Player handbook](../StrategyGuide.md) · [Matchups](../Matchups.md) · [Full mechanics](../../RoulsAscendancy/README.md)

**Leader:** Trent Rou'ls, the Unbodied  
**Playstyle:** Veteran attrition and positional warfare  
**Suggested victories:** Domination; Science as a protected fallback

Build a conventional economy, then turn favourable military kills into Anima. Your advantage is preserving experienced units across a long campaign, provided the shared reserve can cover the casualties you actually take.

## Opening priorities

Improve food and research normally and scout defensible fronts. The capital's small Science bonus does not fund reckless expansion. Fight safe military engagements to establish an Anima reserve before treating reconstruction as insurance.

## Develop the engine

Bring the Choir Eternal and Somatic Lattices into the plan when available. Keep veteran replacements near valid friendly cities and reserve enough Anima for losses before paying for Transmigration. Use a Matriarch to reduce an exchange's cost.

## Convert the advantage

Protect the veterans doing the real work. Choose whether a Modern-era Great Migration is worth exhausting the reserve, and use Buddy to support a coastal campaign rather than expecting a naval aura to solve inland logistics.

## Pros

- Experienced land units can return instead of being replaced by fresh recruits.
- Transmigration can bring a valuable military body to a useful front.
- Later infrastructure improves reserve capacity and reliability.

## Cons and opportunity costs

- The peaceful opening supplies little Anima or broad economic acceleration.
- Reconstruction, movement and special abilities compete for one limited pool.
- Restores are capped per turn; returning units are damaged and cannot immediately act.

## Playing against this civ

- Focus enough targets in one turn to overwhelm the restore cap, then threaten the recovery cities.
- Avoid feeding isolated units into a veteran formation; use range, terrain and a technology lead.
- Exploit an empty reserve after an expensive exchange or special ability.

## Common mistakes

- Spending every Anima point on movement before an enemy attack.
- Treating restoration as unlimited protection for civilians or all unit domains.
- Returning a damaged veteran to combat before it can safely recover.
