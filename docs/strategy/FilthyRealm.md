# The Filthy Realm - strategy guide

[Player handbook](../StrategyGuide.md) · [Matchups](../Matchups.md) · [Full mechanics](../../FilthyRealm/README.md)

**Leader:** Filthy Frank  
**Playstyle:** Foreign-city disruption and timed active control  
**Suggested victories:** Culture with military pressure; Domination as an alternative

Maintain foreign contact to build Filth, earn Filthy Points, and spend them where a temporary disruption changes the next battle or construction race. Contact and a usable treasury of points matter more than the size of a single debuff.

## Opening priorities

Develop a normal cultural economy and identify reachable foreign cities. Maintain safe trade and useful contacts without treating contamination as an immediate army replacement. Build points before planning an expensive active ability.

## Develop the engine

Keep selected targets connected to your pressure so Filth does not simply decay. Use the panel to check valid targets and cooldowns. Match theft, Distortion or Salamander support to a real local objective.

## Convert the advantage

Coordinate It's Time to Stop with your army's position and a decisive turn. Peace Lords and Kitchens strengthen the later loop, but overcommitting to one target can leave your own economy and other fronts exposed.

## Pros

- Several forms of contact can sustain pressure on important foreign cities.
- A shared ability currency allows economic interference or tactical support.
- Late cultural infrastructure supports both Tourism and point generation.

## Cons and opportunity costs

- Contamination takes time and can decay without continued contact.
- Abilities have real costs, range/eligibility rules and cooldowns.
- Economic penalties and a one-turn shutdown do not replace an army capable of exploiting them.

## Playing against this civ

- Reduce avoidable foreign trade exposure and remove hostile units maintaining pressure.
- Force Frank to spend points defensively or divide attention among targets.
- Avoid leaving an entire attack force in range of a single decisive shutdown.

## Common mistakes

- Assuming Filth remains indefinitely after all contact ends.
- Using a costly control ability before friendly units can capitalize.
- Spending points without checking the selected target and cooldown.
