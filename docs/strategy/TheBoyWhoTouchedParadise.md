# Argentina - The Boy Who Touched Paradise - strategy guide

[Player handbook](../StrategyGuide.md) · [Matchups](../Matchups.md) · [Full mechanics](../../TheBoyWhoTouchedParadise/README.md)

**Leader:** Lionel Messi  
**Playstyle:** Great Person culture, national expectations and recovery  
**Suggested victories:** Culture; Science or Diplomacy supported by the Journey

Build a functioning cultural and Great Person economy while managing Dreams and national expectations. El Diez is support for that economy; the Journey's final fulfillment needs genuine achievements and its own eligibility.

## Opening priorities

Develop food and Culture, protect El Diez, and place his hosting-city benefits where Great Person generation is most useful. Establish normal defenses rather than assuming a returning civilian hero can protect a city.

## Develop the engine

Use El Potreros, actual Great Person births, feasible Wonders and diplomatic milestones to earn Dreams. Balance spending with the next Journey requirements. Read the current act and pressure state before delaying a meaningful achievement.

## Convert the advantage

Complete the required national recovery and qualifying achievements, then prepare The Last Dance when its technology, Era and Journey gates are met. Fulfillment improves the cultural plan but does not replace normal victory requirements.

## Pros

- The starting ability supports Great Person generation before the final act.
- El Diez can concentrate inspiration in a selected owned city.
- Several peaceful achievements support Dreams and pressure relief.

## Cons and opportunity costs

- A civilian hero needs safe access and can be unavailable during recovery.
- Journey progress has eligibility and pressure conditions, not just a currency total.
- Purchased or administrative Great Person creation does not count as a genuine generated birth for Dreams.

## Playing against this civ

- Pressure the productive cultural cities and force investment away from Great Person plans.
- Contest realistic Wonder and alliance opportunities rather than gifting easy milestones.
- Threaten the hero's route while recognizing that his loss schedules recovery rather than permanent elimination.

## Common mistakes

- Confusing this Journey with the separate Eternal Number Ten's Legacy career.
- Assuming a purchased Great Person earns Dreams as a generated birth.
- Treating El Diez's voluntary deletion as a way to farm recovery rewards.
