# RomanGladius Network - strategy guide

[Player handbook](../StrategyGuide.md) · [Matchups](../Matchups.md) · [Full mechanics](../../RomanGladiusNetwork/README.md)

**Leader:** Lachlan "RomanGladius"  
**Playstyle:** Population management and a stable wide economy  
**Suggested victories:** Science or Diplomacy; Culture from a mature network

Grow Players, keep staffing proportional to population, and maintain Reputation so the economy can reach higher thresholds. Expansion is valuable only when the new Server can pay its launch and management costs.

## Opening priorities

Grow the capital and establish a treasury before ordering another Server Owner. Read its population and Gold costs before committing. Use the free Console and early milestones to support growth rather than assuming normal Settler economics.

## Develop the engine

Check staff coverage whenever a city crosses a population threshold. Appoint enough Moderators without overmoderating, use an Administrator where its support justifies upkeep, and resolve incidents before weak Reputation becomes a growth spiral.

## Convert the advantage

Protect the largest Servers and keep new cities manageable. Population milestones and accumulated yields can finance conventional research, diplomacy or military production; the dashboard still needs attention during a war.

## Pros

- Population adds Gold, Science and Culture through several thresholds.
- Healthy Reputation can reinforce growth and income.
- Persistent milestones reward developing the network as a whole.

## Cons and opportunity costs

- Server Owners impose unusual population, Production and launch-Gold costs.
- Understaffing and overstaffing can both damage Reputation.
- Low Reputation can slow growth and lead to departures, weakening the yield engine.

## Playing against this civ

- Pressure food tiles and major population centres instead of chasing only peripheral units.
- Force expensive emergency spending when a launch or staffing expansion is planned.
- Take advantage of a troubled Server before its staffing and growth recover.

## Common mistakes

- Applying an ordinary cheap-Settler expansion plan to Server Owners.
- Ignoring the coverage increase after population growth.
- Hiring as many Moderators as possible without checking overmoderation.
