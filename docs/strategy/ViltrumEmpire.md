# Viltrum - strategy guide

[Player handbook](../StrategyGuide.md) · [Matchups](../Matchups.md) · [Full mechanics](../../ViltrumEmpire/README.md)

**Leader:** Grand Regent Thragg  
**Playstyle:** Conquest momentum with an unavoidable biological crisis  
**Suggested victories:** Domination; rebuild before pursuing a conventional fallback

Turn first foreign conquests into momentum while preparing a state that can survive the Scourge. The elite Warrior is a timing opportunity and a crisis trigger; reaching its technology is not the end of the campaign's planning.

## Opening priorities

Develop a productive economy, garrisons and a conventional army. Choose early conquests for lasting value, not recapture farming. Growth and Happiness matter because the later biological crisis will damage both population and military capacity.

## Develop the engine

Use first-conquest Momentum to support a coherent campaign. Before the Warrior and outbreak timing, build recovery infrastructure, retain conventional Auxiliaries and avoid making every front depend on Bloodline survivors.

## Convert the advantage

Choose Quarantine or Crusade with the current army, food, treasury and enemy pressure in mind. Defend the weak survivors, respect the restrictions of the selected phase, and rebuild production before attempting another long offensive.

## Pros

- First conquests can reinforce military Production and experience.
- Elite Warriors provide powerful late operational tools.
- Garrisons and support infrastructure contribute to occupation and recovery.

## Cons and opportunity costs

- The Scourge is guaranteed and cannot be solved by avoiding one optional panel.
- Both crisis routes impose severe population and economic losses.
- Kills, conquests and returns have attribution and once-per-city limits; they are not free farms.

## Playing against this civ

- Avoid feeding a chain of undefended first conquests.
- Prepare pressure for the actual crisis and recovery phase rather than meeting the elite army at its strongest moment.
- Force several defensive commitments when production, population and survivors are weakest.

## Common mistakes

- Assuming the Scourge can be skipped by delaying the first Warrior indefinitely.
- Leaving every defensive duty to Bloodline units.
- Choosing Crusade without enough targets, reserves and safe healing for its survivors.
