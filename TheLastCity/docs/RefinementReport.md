# Last City refinement report — 2026-10-10

This is an incremental correction of the existing civilization, Council, persistence and SQL architecture. The Warden remains human-selectable and excluded from normal AI selection. All original survival systems and the narrative endgame remain connected. Collection v21 and standalone v1 retain their GUIDs; survival snapshot schema advances from 1 to 2.

## Mechanics and performance improved

`LastCityEconomy.Stats` and Council ration previews include infection and blockade consumption. Pillaged/repaired improvement income, capacity, destroyed/rebuilt Housing contributions and expert effects are tested against actual SQL. Growth uses hundredths of native surplus, retaining fractional adjustments; native city growth has already executed before `PlayerDoTurn`. It cannot undo growth already awarded at a threshold. Negative adjustments are clamped to retained Food; nonpositive surplus grants no generous Food.

The six expert roles retain their effects and assignment/reserve caps. Percentage bonuses still use desired dummy-building counts and mutually exclusive promotions; reconciliation does not add repeated percentages. Casualty deduplication retains only relevant living/delayed-unit tokens, and participant identity reuses existing unit tokens instead of accumulating one script tag per siege.

`LastCityCore.Changed` refreshes the army only when defense inputs change or ranks need reconciliation. Identical snapshots skip writes. `SanctuaryCouncil` reuses its seven tab instances, invalidates stale previews on changed state, counts overlapping popup blockers and responds to interface-mode changes. It remains event-driven, with no frame timer, full-map UI search or continuous combat polling.

`LastCityAI.AITurn` evaluates resources after assigning experts, prioritizes supply/Housing needs and eligible Dawn construction, and preserves ongoing production. Only an active siege with an inadequate army can interrupt a building, with a scaled intervention cooldown; Dawn construction is protected. Native AI still handles garrison movement and diplomacy. The fallback is tested even though ordinary AI selection of this civ is disabled.

Original generated Dawn of Man art is compiled to a registered 1024×512 DXT5 DDS. All other atlas/model references remain valid inherited assets. [Art.md](Art.md) contains the final prompt, source path, tool/skill and reproducible conversion command.

- [CvGame.cpp](https://github.com/LoneGazebo/Community-Patch-DLL/blob/Release-5.4.6/CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvGame.cpp), [CvPlayer.cpp](https://github.com/LoneGazebo/Community-Patch-DLL/blob/Release-5.4.6/CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CvPlayer.cpp): engine update invokes `testAlive`; `verifyAlive` eliminates a player with no cities/units even under Complete Kills, and the engine updates game state. The implementation relies on this native path rather than `SetAlive` or a fake screen.
- [CustomMods.h](https://github.com/LoneGazebo/Community-Patch-DLL/blob/Release-5.4.6/CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/CustomMods.h), native call sites and [Lua/CvLuaUnit.cpp](https://github.com/LoneGazebo/Community-Patch-DLL/blob/Release-5.4.6/CvGameCoreDLL_Expansion2/Lua/CvLuaUnit.cpp): signatures, return semantics and numeric mission/queue arguments audited. The full contract table is in [Implementation.md](Implementation.md). `UnitPillageGold` is a VALUE event and can be evaluated during calculation; infection deliberately uses bounded siege proximity instead of side effects in that callback or global battle observers.

`Modding.OpenSaveData` is documented as savegame storage rather than global `OpenUserData`; its proprietary engine implementation is not contained in the CP DLL. The native checksum marker additionally detects missing or mismatched bank state. Different-game and earlier-slot behavior is mocked, not claimed as a native save-slot test. A stale backup is intentionally disabled, requiring an intact save rather than risking duplicate rewards.

Native movement/combat AI may still redirect units. Expanded/owned searches exclude all other players' land; if every legal local approach is blocked/occupied, blockades continue until a physical encounter is possible. A blockade never counts toward twelve invasion victories or completes Dawn. Original-capital/holy-city history can change when an exceptional orphan is dismantled by native cleanup. Unsupported mods replacing city/unit ownership or overwriting native ScriptData may need additional compatibility work.

Humanity Endures remains a narrative achievement with Endless Survival, not a native victory type. Ordinary victory rules remain available. No additional DLL dependency is introduced.

## Balance changes

- Military collapse: at least two tracked enemies within two plots, one adjacent, no nearby combat defender, at least 85% native maximum city damage, three scaled consecutive sanctuary turns. Reinforcement or repair resets it; thresholds are configurable.
- Blockade: six scaled turns, supply drain `max(1, 6 + floor(era/2) + (final and 2 or 0) - defense)`, where up to three stationed defenders and two assigned Veterans mitigate it. Morale -0.6/speed per turn and at most one additional shelter damage when local defense is insufficient. Physical retry four scaled turns after ending; no fabricated victory.
- Plague: severity 1–4, at most eight scaled turns and four scaled recovery-immunity turns, no stacking/refresh exploit. Medical protection reduces chance/severity/duration/drain; severe untreated mortality is limited to 1.5%/speed per turn and never below one Population. Outbreak treatment clears infection.
- Gradual ration/crowding/recovery Morale rates and starvation mortality probabilities normalize by speed. Standard rates are retained; slower games no longer multiply cumulative attrition between encounters. Times/lump supplies use GrowthPercent, crisis Production uses ConstructPercent, and native building scaling is applied once. Mixed custom-speed fields are explicitly tested.
- Outskirts raids are capped at three ordinary attackers/five Major Siege attackers; a Final Night still requires the planned-force eligibility check. Confirmed boss death adds the previously missing +6 Morale.
- Original costs, initial resources, expert caps, normal schedules, permanent defense cap and Dawn prerequisites remain.
