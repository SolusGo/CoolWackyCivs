# Original CR7 artwork

`RelentlessJourney.png` was generated with Codex's built-in imagegen tool for this mod. `ArtPreview.png` previews the compiled composition and original programmatic icons. No downloaded photographs or protected club logos were used. The static leader/Dawn scene shares that original composition; its portrait is cropped for circular-sized selection icons. The route map and emblem/symbol atlases are original code-drawn art compiled by `tools/make_ronaldo_assets.py`.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: Civilization V original leader and Dawn of Man game artwork. Create a cinematic 16:9 painted digital illustration of Cristiano Ronaldo as a confident disciplined veteran Portuguese football captain in a plain deep red jersey with small golden number 7, no official emblems or brands. Waist-up heroic portrait, face recognizable, short dark hair, focused calm expression, stadium floodlights behind him. Background is a cohesive sweeping journey montage: Madeira coastal hillside, Lisbon, Manchester industrial stadium silhouette, Madrid classical architecture, Portugal coastline, then abstract golden horizon expressing ongoing longevity. Deep Portuguese red, gold, green, white palette. Polished historically inspired Civ V loading-screen painting, dramatic golden rim lighting, rich atmospheric detail. No text other than the small number 7, no watermarks, no club logos. Landscape wide composition suitable as a leader screen and dawn loading image.

DDS files use DXT5 for block-aligned sizes and uncompressed RGBA for 45-pixel icons. Atlases register all 256/128/80/64/48/45/32/24/16 sizes. Leader icons register 256/128/64; the transparent number-seven flag is 32 pixels.
