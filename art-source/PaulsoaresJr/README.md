# First Night art provenance and prompts

Four original illustrations were generated with the built-in imagegen tool. These files are sources, not shipped textures. `tools/make_psj_assets.py` only crops/resizes the illustration sources, packs atlases and adds conventional gold UI rims. Torch/house, torch/book and compass UI marks are drawn as simple native geometry for readable alpha masks and tiny icons. Preview: `ArtPreview.png`.

References used for inspiration (not shipped as mod content):

- `ProfileReference.jpg`: [Paul's public YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@PSJR), current public avatar retrieved 5 October 2026, featuring the square grey-bearded explorer with olive hat/shirt.
- `FirstNightReference.jpg`: [early Minecraft tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7MqkEZn8pN4), thumbnail showing the simple Alpha world and punching the first tree. Used as environment/mood reference.

Generation prompt set (built-in tool, not CLI):

- **Leader.png:** Civilization V landscape static diplomacy scene; recognizable avatar from the profile reference, square face, grey beard, friendly eyes, olive hat/shirt; patient guide holding a torch beside a crude dirt/cobblestone shelter at dusk; polished painterly texture, warm amber and cool blue, unexplored green hills; centered complete head suitable for portrait cropping; no text, logo, UI, border or collage.
- **Dawn.png:** 16:9 painterly early Alpha world; tiny awkward cube of dirt/cobblestone with wood door, torch and glowing window; safe first shelter in a vast unknown valley, dark cavern, stepped hills, block trees, sunset into starry night; no people, monsters, text, border or elaborate modern house.
- **Survivor.png:** square unit portrait source; young curious voxel explorer in olive jacket and canvas backpack holding a torch, ordinary tools, cautious journey into dark blue woods; fragile explorer, no sword/armor; large readable central silhouette, amber/twilight palette; no text, rim or UI.
- **House.png:** square building portrait source; ugly beloved flat-roof cube of dirt/cobblestone, wood door, glowing window and torch on grass hill; simple clear central silhouette, warm safe light in blue twilight; painterly voxel forms; no elaborate roof, text, rim or UI.

Final sizes and format details are in `../../PaulsoaresJr/README.md`. Art is fan tribute imagery and is not represented as official Minecraft or PaulsoaresJr artwork. No music, narration or voice samples are included.
