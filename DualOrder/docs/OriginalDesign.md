# Original design — The Dual Order

**Civilization:** The Dual Order

**Leader:** Grandmaster Severin

**Theme:** A militant religious order that maintains equilibrium between Faith and military power.

**Preferred victory:** Domination supported by religion; Culture secondary.

**Start bias:** Hills.
**Motto:** “Faith Governs. Steel Ensures.”

## Twin Mandates

Barracks, Armories, Temples, and equivalent unique buildings provide +1 Production and +1 Faith. Units trained in a city containing both military-training and religious infrastructure receive Zeal. Zeal grants +10% Combat Strength inside or adjacent to friendly territory and heals 10 HP after a kill; it persists through upgrades.

While at war and generating positive Faith per turn, Balance Pressure grants +2% Combat Strength and +1% Production in all Cities per civilization currently at war with the Dual Order, capped at five simultaneous wars. During Golden Ages, the empire receives +25% Production toward military units and trained units receive +5 XP.

## Divided Templar

Replaces the Longswordsman with 23 Combat Strength, normal Movement, 10% higher Production cost, and the normal Iron requirement. It begins with Cover I. Schism Strike grants +20% Combat Strength when attacking a unit below 50% HP and +10% Combat Strength while adjacent to a Great Prophet, Missionary, or Inquisitor.

## Hall of Concordance

Replaces the Armory. It retains normal Armory effects and adds +3 Faith, +2 Production, +1 Happiness when the City follows the Dual Order's founded religion, and five additional XP. The requested fallback for the combat-origin Great General mechanic is +1 Great General Point per turn.

## Dawn of Man

> Grandmaster, the Dual Order awaits your command. For generations, faith and steel have stood together, neither permitted to dominate the other. The faithful give purpose to the warrior, and the warrior gives protection to the faithful.
>
> Build halls where priest and soldier stand as equals. Let your armies march with conviction, and let every conflict strengthen the bond upon which your realm was founded.
>
> Grandmaster Severin, can you preserve the balance of the Twin Mandates and forge an Order that will endure the test of time?
